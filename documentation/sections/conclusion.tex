\paragraph{Future Work.} Several directions merit further investigation:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Extended evaluation}: Testing across multiple market regimes, including bear markets and high-volatility periods.
    
    \item \textbf{Advanced architectures}: Exploring transformer-based models and attention mechanisms for temporal dependencies.
    
    \item \textbf{Multi-objective optimization}: Incorporating risk constraints directly into the RL objective.
    
    \item \textbf{Transfer learning}: Pre-training on traditional financial markets and fine-tuning on cryptocurrency data.
    
    \item \textbf{Online learning}: Adapting to non-stationary market dynamics through continual learning approaches.
\end{enumerate}
